1. Introduction
Purpose
This project aims to build a production-style cryptocurrency exchange backend inspired by Binance using FastAPI. The objective is to gain hands-on experience designing scalable backend systems, implementing secure authentication, managing wallets, processing orders, building a matching engine, and exposing real-time APIs.
The application is intended for educational purposes only and will not interact with real cryptocurrencies or real financial assets.

What the verifier checks
Every test drives the game in a browser. It reads what a player would see: the ship's position, whether a run ends, whether a pickup registers, and where HUD elements sit on screen. No test reads source code or checks variable names.
Checks
Progress reaches at least 95% when the ship reaches the finish gate.
The run ends at the gate. The ship overshoots by no more than 40px.
After a retry, progress resets to 0-2% at spawn. It reads 45-55% at the halfway point.
After a level clear, the next level's progress also resets to 0%. It reads 45-55% at its halfway point.
All three stars on levels 1-5 sit inside the flyable canyon. None are stuck in the rock.
Flying through each star collects it. The counter reaches 3/3.
The ship survives near the ceiling when there is open space above it.
At 1024px and 900px widths, all four HUD buttons stay on screen and clickable.
The progress percentage does not overlap the star or coin counters.
The HUD strip is tall enough. Nothing inside it gets clipped.
The ship still survives a close pass near the floor (must keep working).
Flying through a coin still collects it (must keep working).
The HUD stays hidden until Play is pressed, then appears (must keep working).
Pausing freezes the ship. It drifts less than 1px over 350ms (must keep working).
Why these checks prove the fix
The broken app has five defects. The finish gate does not stop the run, so the ship overshoots and progress reads low at the finish line. The progress formula breaks after a retry or level clear, so it can show 0% mid-run. Stars sit on the wrong side of the canyon centerline, placing them in the rock. The ceiling's collision margin is too generous, killing the ship in open space. The HUD panel is too wide, pushing buttons off screen at narrow widths.
Each check targets one defect. Fixing that value, and only that value, makes the matching check pass.
The four checks marked "must keep working" cover floor collision, coin pickup, HUD visibility, and pause. The fix does not touch any of these. They confirm the fix breaks nothing that already worked.
What these tests do not check: source code, function names, audio, exact colors or fonts, frame rate, or every level in the game. The star check covers levels 1-5 as a sample, not the full set.
